# Welcome to My Academic Portfolio

I am a PhD Researcher in Digital Healthcare at Wonkwang University, South Korea.

My research focuses on artificial intelligence, speech processing, and multimodal approaches for digital healthcare, with a particular interest in the early detection of neurological disorders.

## Research Interests

- Speech Processing and Automatic Speech Recognition
- AI-Based Healthcare and Disease Detection
- Alzheimer's Disease and Parkinson's Disease
- Multimodal Learning and Foundation Models
- Medical Image Analysis and Computer Vision

## Current Research

### Speech and Language Analysis for Healthcare

Exploring AI methods to identify speech and language patterns associated with neurological disorders.

### Multimodal Artificial Intelligence

Investigating approaches that combine speech, language, and other relevant health data for disease analysis.

### Medical Image Analysis

Researching deep learning methods for medical image segmentation and classification.

## Publications

My research includes work in medical image analysis, skin lesion classification, and AI-based emotion recognition.
